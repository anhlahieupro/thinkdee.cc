2. How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your Apple subscription purchased through an iOS app, follow these steps:
1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap on your Apple ID name at the top of the screen.
3. Select Subscriptions.
4. Find the subscription you want to cancel and tap on it.
5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm your cancellation.

Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
